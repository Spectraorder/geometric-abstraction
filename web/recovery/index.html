    Inspect the draft
    

    Completeness needs a declared scope.
    A successful video is one data point. A policy needs coverage, recovery, and honest failure handling.
    Task-level competence
    Measure nominal and randomized completion, shape identity, clutter handling, transient recovery, and correct abstention. Use a private evaluator with frozen policy and harness hashes.
    Release readiness
    Every required cell must meet coverage and confidence targets. Missing tests stay incomplete. Failed, timed-out, and infrastructure attempts remain in the report.
    Suggested release protocol
    At least 50 independent scenes per cell; a 95% Wilson lower bound of at least 0.90; zero observed unsafe actions. Repeats are clustered by scene. These are proposed release criteria, not results or universal guarantees.
    Declare tower-recovery, inventory, or all-supported scope. Separately attest sensor-only inputs, frozen versions, heldout isolation, bounded execution, complete accounting, private evaluation, and action safety.

    What would count as self-correction?
    Preserve the abnormal observation, escalation trigger, before/after plan, fresh observation, resumed actions, and final private evaluation. Two model calls alone do not prove a useful correction.
    Do not equate confidence with calibrated action success. Do not use Astra's answer as ground truth. Report model wait, simulation time, wall time, raw decision errors, and guard interventions separately.
