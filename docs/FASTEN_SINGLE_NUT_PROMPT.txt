PHYSICS AND SCORING CONTRACT
This uses original RoboDojo nut/bolt meshes in our simplified MuJoCo
helical-constraint simulation, not RoboDojo's PhysX SDF collision model.
Entry requires an actual two-jaw grasp, upright nut axis within 6 degrees,
bore centered within 2 mm, and the nut bottom around 2 mm below the bolt tip
(within 2 mm). This engages the thread; no special attach API exists.
Both parts use the original benchmark surfaces. The simplified coupling seats
the nut at the modeled thread phase for its yaw, within half a pitch of nominal
entry; depth is measured from this phase-specific entry. Individual thread faces
are not collision-resolved, and cross-threading is not modeled.
The current V2 model uses a solver-resolved screw joint and a nut-only entry
shoulder. An unengaged nut cannot freely slide down the shaft. Once engaged,
it resists sideways pulling and axial motion inconsistent with rotation.
The nut is never attached to the gripper: jaws can slip or lose contact.
Engagement may occur during a low alignment correction if the entry conditions
are already satisfied. Do not assume it is unengaged simply because your code
has not reached its intended entry phase; pulling it back can fail tracking.
Use visual motion to detect this situation, release safely, or reverse to unthread.
After entry, a full clockwise revolution lowers the nut by 3 mm.
At least 3 net clockwise turns and 9 mm depth from entry are required.
Track actual object rotation visually: wrist rotation without nut rotation does
not count, oscillating back and forth does not accumulate turns, and pushing
straight down cannot count. Regrasp between partial turns because wrist roll is
limited. Thread friction holds an engaged nut during release/regrasp.
The model retains V3's solver-based static friction: waiting or releasing the nut
must not be used to advance fastening. Earlier V1/V2 turn counts could include
gravity-driven drift and are not proof of robot-driven rotation in this model.
Wrong-color engagement cannot earn credit. Nut must remain upright and centered,
free of robot contacts, with rotation consistent with depth for at least 2s.
Score 0: none, or gripper not open/released.
Score 20: one matching pair fastened and gripper open.
This one-pair curriculum succeeds when that pair is fastened, gripper open,
and arm at initial home (each joint within .12 rad). Its score remains 20;
this is not completion of the three-pair benchmark. There are no other pairs.
A separate evaluator waits 5s and checks these conditions. It is never queried
during the run. Stage counts and measured turns/depth are post-trial feedback only.

SENSORS

Reconstruct points as world=R@(inv(K)@[u,v,1]*depth[v,u])+t.
Camera axes right/down/forward. Use segmentation, geometry and connected regions
to distinguish the flat annular nut from the upright shaft/head of the same color.
api.camera(view='wrist') returns RGB plus calibration, NO depth.
api.proprioception() returns five joint_angles, tool_position world XYZ,
tool_rotation 3x3 and time. This is robot kinematics, not an object oracle.

MOTION
