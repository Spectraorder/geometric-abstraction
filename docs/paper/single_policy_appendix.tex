\paragraph{Evaluation notes.}
Bridge and Stacking require the prescribed support structure and a five-second
stable hold. Ring requires the peg through the opening, the ring center below
the peg top, release from robot contact, and two seconds of stability. Invalid
programs count as failures. Stacking expanded from 20 to 100 test layouts without
retraining. Bridge/Stacking's contact checks can miss unnamed robot collision
geometry; Ring uses body-ancestry checks. Ring's 12\,mm lateral clearance defines
a placement task, not precision insertion.
